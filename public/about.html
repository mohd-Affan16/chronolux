The Maison

Founded in the heart of the Swiss Alps in 1892, ChronoLux represents the absolute pinnacle of haute horlogerie. We do not simply tell time; we encapsulate it within masterpieces of micro-engineering.

The Philosophy

Every timepiece is hand-assembled by a single master watchmaker over the course of six months. We utilize only the rarest materials: aerospace-grade titanium, 18-karat brushed rose gold, and meteorites forged in the birth of the solar system.

Our commitment is to precision, longevity, and the subtle art of mechanical perfection.
